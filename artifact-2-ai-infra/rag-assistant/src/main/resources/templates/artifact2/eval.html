Metrics explained:
• Retrieval hit rate: fraction of questions where the expected source doc appears in top-K results.
• Answer accuracy: fraction where ≥50% of expected keywords appear in the generated answer.
• Citation validity: fraction where every cited chunk ID exists in the retrieved set (catches hallucinated citations).
• Precision@K / Recall@K: retrieval quality measures.
